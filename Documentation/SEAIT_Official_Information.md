## 3. Institutional Timeline & Historical Milestones

- **2006 — Birth of SEAIT**: Conceived by Mr. & Mrs. Reynaldo S. Tamayo Jr. Initial TESDA offerings: 2-Year Diploma in Computer Programming NC IV and Computer Hardware Servicing NC II.
- **2007 — TESDA Expansion**: Granted authorization for 2-Year Diploma in Hotel and Restaurant Management.
- **2008 — Initial CHED Program**: Commission on Higher Education granted authorization for Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT).
- **2009 — Business & Hospitality**: CHED granted laddered B.S. in Hotel Management and B.S. in Business Administration major in Marketing Management.
- **2011 — Education & Engineering Expansion**: Launched B.S. in Secondary Education (English & Math), B.S. in Elementary Education, and B.S. in Civil Engineering.
- **2013 — Criminal Justice**: Introduction of Bachelor of Science in Criminology.
- **2014 — Agriculture**: CHED granted authorization for Bachelor of Science in Agriculture.
- **2016 — Basic Education**: Government recognition from DepEd for the K-12 Program (SHS & JHS).
- **2019 — Fisheries & Early Childhood**: Added B.S. in Fisheries (CAF) and B.S. in Early Childhood Education (CTE).
- **2020 — Public & Social Sciences**: Added B.S. in Social Work, B.S.Ed. major in Social Studies, and Bachelor of Agricultural Technology.
- **2022 — Agriculture & Sciences Expansion**: Specializations in Animal Science and Crop Science (BSA); B.S.Ed. majors in Filipino and General Science.
- **2023 — Technology Education**: Added Bachelor of Technology and Livelihood Education (BTLEd) major in ICT.
- **2024–2026 — Engineering & Computing Expansion**: Introduction of Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering (BSEE) and BSIT Business Analytics.

## 8. Official Student Admission Flow (Portal Benchmark)

1. **Step 1: Application Form**: Submission of complete applicant demographic profile and program preference.
2. **Step 2: Document Intake & Checklist**: Submission of SHS diploma/report cards, PSA birth certificate, certificate of good moral character.
3. **Step 3: SEAIT College Admission Test (SCAT)**: General aptitude evaluation & department-specific cutoff exam (especially for CCJE Criminology and BSSW Social Work board tracks).
4. **Step 4: Interview & Evaluation**: Academic load advisement and interview by the College Dean / Program Head / Assigned Faculty Evaluator.
5. **Step 5: Official Enrollment & Confirmation**: Registrar validation, ID card issuance, and freshman orientation scheduling.
